Area uses Tiny Tapeout's rules of thumb for a 1x1 tile, about 160×100 µm: about 320 DFFs, or up to about 512 latch bits, with no room left for logic. The scan budget assumes the chip reads one segment per clock into a line buffer, with 1056 clocks per scanline at 40 MHz and the cell height in scanlines per cell row.

Settings

Implicit wraps. Each entry stores the corner (x, y) and the sign of the direction it leaves in. Turns always alternate between horizontal and vertical, so the axis comes for free. A straight segment can wrap at most once before hitting its own start. That means a segment from A to B that moves "backwards" must have wrapped, so wraps need no entries.
